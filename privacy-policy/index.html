Privacy policy

This policy explains information shared when you visit prairiegloss.shop, contact Prairie Gloss Mobile Detailing, or request an appointment.

Last updated: October 2, 2026.

Home›Privacy policy

Information you share

When you call or request an appointment, you may provide your name, contact details, appointment location, vehicle details, preferred time, and information about the service you need. Please share only information relevant to your request.

This website does not have an account-registration form or collect payment details directly. Online booking opens a separate Cal.com page.

How information is used

Information supplied with an inquiry or booking is used to respond to you, discuss service suitability, arrange the appointment, and communicate about the requested work. If you have questions about information associated with your appointment, contact us using the details below.

Website services and external links

Vercel hosts this website. When your browser requests a page or asset, the hosting provider may process technical information such as your IP address, browser details, requested URL, and request time to deliver and operate the site.

The homepage loads fonts from Google Fonts. Those requests connect your browser to Google's servers. Booking links open Cal.com, and Google review links open Google. Information you provide on those services is also subject to their own privacy policies:

  • Vercel privacy policy
  • Cal.com privacy policy
  • Google privacy policy

Cookies and browser settings

This site's own code does not set cookies or use advertising trackers. External services may use cookies or similar technologies when you visit them. You can manage cookies through your browser settings and review the settings provided by those services.

Privacy questions and requests

To ask about information you have shared with Prairie Gloss, or to request a correction or deletion, call 956-300-4705. We will discuss your request and any information needed to identify the relevant appointment. Records that must be kept for legal or business obligations may need to be retained.
